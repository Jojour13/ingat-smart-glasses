The Memory Vault

One vault for each person in his life. Some of it the family typed in. The rest it worked out by being there — which is the point, because nobody has time to fill in a form about their own daughter.

What is kept, and what is not. A finished conversation leaves one record: who, when, how long, what it was about, and anything a family member read and approved. It does not leave a transcript, a recording, or a quote. You can open last Tuesday and see what the visit was about. Nobody — not the family, not us, not a court order — can open last Tuesday and read what was said, because those words were never written down.
