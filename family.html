Add a memory

One of these is played each afternoon after 3pm, rotating. Late afternoon is when restlessness builds. Across fourteen randomised trials, reminiscence therapy showed a significant cognitive effect only when delivered one-to-one — which is the thing a device can do and a rota of visitors cannot.

optional, high value

A familiar voice is recognised faster than a synthetic one, is harder to ignore, and is calming rather than clinical. Record the line you would actually say.

His day

Not a to-do list — a to-do list has to be remembered, which is the whole problem. Each of these is spoken at the moment it matters, once, and never again if he has already done it.

Add to his day

A good day has gaps in it

Four or five things is plenty. A packed day is not a supportive day — it is a day full of moments to fail at, and every prompt he does not act on teaches him that the glasses are noise. If in doubt, take one out.

Things he puts down and cannot find

He asks the glasses “where is my wallet?” and they answer. Give each thing a home — where it belongs — and the glasses fall back to that whenever they have not seen it recently.

The six that matter in dementia are almost always the same: keys, wallet, phone, walking stick, spectacles case, TV remote. A UWB tag on each makes the answer exact and works inside a drawer; untagged things fall back to their home.

Safe zone

not set

Not a map with a dot on it. A circle around home: if he leaves it you are told, and he is told which way home is. That is the difference between watching him and helping him.

Needs a real location fix, so do this on the phone, outdoors or by a window. Location is a separate consent — grant it in the Trust Centre.

Trackers

checking…

Clip a Bluetooth tag to the things he loses. When he asks “where is my wallet?” the glasses ring the tag and tell him where to listen. A sound finds a wallet under a cushion faster than any description.

Add a tracker

Your browser will show its own list of nearby devices — we never see anything you do not choose. Pairing has to be started by a tap, every time; a web page is not allowed to scan quietly in the background, which is exactly as it should be.
